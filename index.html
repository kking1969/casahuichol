One night, under the stars, with mezcal made in front of you, music from a legend, and the art and traditions of the people who carry them forward.

Live Ancestral Mezcal Distillation

Award-winning Maestro Mezcalero Fredy Chávez comes from San Pedro Totomachápam, Oaxaca, to distill mezcal live in the garden the ancestral way: clay pots and a wood fire, drop by drop. Your ticket includes a shot poured straight from the still.

Live Music by Ras Klamps of The Original Wailers

Ras Klamps, keyboardist of The Original Wailers from Jamaica, performs live.

Wixárika Blessing

The night opens with a blessing led by Maracame Micaela López de la Cruz of the Wixárika community.

Art & Textiles

  • Evencio Vicente Ruiz, master Zapotec weaver from Teotitlán del Valle, Oaxaca, with his collection of hand-dyed, handwoven textile art Tierra Zapoteca
  • Backstrap-loom huipiles from Pinotepa de Don Luis, Oaxaca, Ikal_apparel
  • Wixárika art by Micaela Lopez de la Cruz and Santos Hernández

Mezcal, Cocktails & Local Cuisine

  • Craft mezcal cocktails by @Tropical_Spirit_Experience
  • An ancestral mezcal sale for bottle collectors by Así Me Enseñaron
  • Birria specialties and postres from Restaurante Anita (not included with the ticket)

Your Ticket Includes

  • 1 craft cocktail OR 1 beer
  • 1 shot of mezcal fresh from the live still
  • 1 handmade quesadilla with chapulines, provided by our sponsor Restaurante Anita

All in Support of Casa Huichol

A portion of the night's proceeds goes directly to Casa Huichol.
