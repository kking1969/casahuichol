Casa Huichol

Supporting Wixárika Families, Culture & Traditions

Casa Huichol is a nonprofit organization founded in 1983 that provides essential healthcare support to the Wixárika (Huichol) Indigenous communities of Mexico.

Based in the Guadalajara area, the organization helps families from remote mountain villages access medical treatment by providing free lodging, meals, transportation, medical assistance, and support during hospital visits.

Beyond healthcare, Casa Huichol is dedicated to preserving the rich cultural traditions of the Wixárika people.

Through donations and fundraising events, the organization continues to make a meaningful difference in the lives of Indigenous families who might otherwise struggle to access critical medical care.
